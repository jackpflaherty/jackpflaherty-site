Jack P. Flaherty

About

Jack Flaherty is the trusted advisor who helps boards and executives make decisions with technology.

For 25 years in risk and advisory, 15 of them at the Big 4, Jack has sat in the rooms where technology decisions get made, defended and paid for. He has watched capable leaders stall on calls they were equipped to make, and he has watched the ones who gave the decision an owner move faster than everyone else.

That observation became a method: treat new technology like a new hire. A job description, a manager, a probation period, a review. The method became one keynote, Fear Is Not A Control, built for health-system leaders and professional services partners, and the same questions he asks a board when a new system is about to go live.

Where he has sat

  • Acting enterprise risk and audit leader for a $10B health system, reporting to the C-suite and the audit committee.
  • Led a board-mandated cybersecurity programme for a $4B health system: built the board proposal, hired and managed the vendors, and closed the urgent gaps on a $2.5 million budget within one year.
  • Data governance implementation for a $15B financial institution.
  • Principal consultant for California’s first statewide health information exchange.
  • Ran PwC’s national Healthcare Risk Roundtable, a gathering of C-suite risk executives, twice a year for five years.
  • Discreet board-requested investigations.
  • Interim COO and risk advisor for fintech, digital-asset and growth-stage companies through Connacht Ventures.

How he got here

Jack started on the derivatives trading desks in New York, then moved to Los Angeles, finished a one-year MBA at Pepperdine and joined Deloitte’s risk advisory practice in 2001. He moved from financial services into healthcare, implementing and auditing enterprise and clinical systems, and led training for the Southern California HIMSS chapter.

A layoff in 2009 became his first consulting firm, Abidance Solutions. He joined Orion Health because he saw data integration as the next thing in healthcare, then returned to the Big 4 at PwC as a director focused on healthcare risk and digital assurance.

He wrote The Decision Switch after watching leaders outside the Big 4 and the regulated industries struggle with decisions about technology that his clients had learned to make, and he began speaking. In 2026 he rebuilt his platform under his own name.

Speaking and writing

  • Keynote: Fear Is Not A Control, 45 to 60 minutes, for health-system executives and nurse leaders and for partners in accounting, advisory and law firms.
  • Author of The Decision Switch (Cinch & Stone Press).
  • Has spoken at HIMSS, Deloitte regional events, PwC’s national Healthcare Risk Roundtable, the IIA, ISACA, AHIA, Tenable, NCSBN and the Nursing World Conference.
  • Writes The Clear Confidence Report. The site’s Insights page holds the pieces that sell the keynote.

Credentials

  • Held CISSP, CISA and CGEIT.
  • Board member, Risso Foundation, since 2015, funding strategies for Hispanic students.
  • Based in Los Angeles.
