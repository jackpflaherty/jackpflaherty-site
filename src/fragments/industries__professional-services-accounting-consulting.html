Questions partners and planners ask

Who is this for?

Partners and practice leaders in accounting, advisory and law firms, and the people who build those firms. Managing partners, practice heads, risk and quality leaders, and the executive committees they report to.

Is it an AI talk?

AI is in scope, but the talk is about any new technology that reaches an engagement or a client: an AI tool, a new platform, a system the client adopted first. The method is the same for each.

Does Jack customise the keynote?

Every time. Every booking starts with a 30-minute discovery call about who is in the room and what decision they are facing. No proposal goes out before that call.

Live or virtual?

Both. The virtual version runs live on Zoom or Teams and is rebuilt for the screen, not transferred from the stage.

Is there a workshop?

Yes. A half-day working session after the keynote, where partners and practice leaders apply the new-hire method to a technology decision they are facing now. It is quoted on the discovery call.

My event is close. Is it too late?

Ask. Jack keeps limited availability for short-notice dates and will say quickly whether the date and the room fit. Email jack@jackpflaherty.com directly.
