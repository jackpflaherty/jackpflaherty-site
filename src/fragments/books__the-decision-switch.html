The Decision Switch

Decision-making is a process, not an event. The Decision Switch is Jack P. Flaherty’s book on how leaders make the call under pressure and stand behind it. Cinch & Stone Press.

Built from 25 years in risk and advisory and the rooms where decisions carried the highest cost, the seven principles show you how to:

  • Filter the noise. Triage what is in front of you so the decision gets made before fatigue makes it for you.
  • Keep your focus. Align priorities, effort and decisions with the mission, not the loudest voice in the room.
  • Build bridges. Bring the people who disagree into the decision before it is made, not after.
  • Clear your vision. Use objective inquiry to spot the biases that cloud judgement.
  • Build momentum. Give the decision a champion who keeps it moving and accountable.
  • Build trust. Recognise the sacrifices and setbacks a decision costs the people who carry it.
  • Learn the lesson. Turn every result, good or bad, into the next better decision.

It is a how-to guide, not a theory. The same method now underpins Jack’s keynote, Fear Is Not A Control, and the advice he gives boards when a new system is about to go live. See the keynote or read the Insights built on it.
