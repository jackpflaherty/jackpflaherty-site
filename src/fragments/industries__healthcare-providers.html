Questions meeting planners ask

Who is this for?

Health-system executives, nurse leaders and the clinical and operational teams who decide which technology reaches the unit. Chief nursing officers, CIOs, CMIOs, quality and risk leaders, and the boards they report to.

Is it an AI talk?

AI is in scope, but the talk is about any new technology that reaches a patient: an AI tool, a monitoring system, a new module in the record. The method is the same for each.

Does Jack customise the keynote?

Every time. Every booking starts with a 30-minute discovery call about who is in the room and what decision they are facing. No proposal goes out before that call.

Live or virtual?

Both. The virtual version runs live on Zoom or Teams and is rebuilt for the screen, not transferred from the stage.

Is there a workshop?

Yes. A half-day working session after the keynote, where clinical and operational teams apply the new-hire method to a technology decision they are facing now. It is quoted on the discovery call.

My event is close. Is it too late?

Ask. Jack keeps limited availability for short-notice dates and will say quickly whether the date and the room fit. Email jack@jackpflaherty.com directly.
