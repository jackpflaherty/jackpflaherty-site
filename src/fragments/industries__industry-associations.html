Questions programme chairs ask

Which audiences does this fit?

Health-system and nursing associations, accounting, advisory and legal professional bodies, audit and risk associations, and any conference where members make technology decisions they are accountable for.

Is it an AI talk?

AI is in scope, but the talk is about any new technology that lands on a member’s desk. The method is the same for each, which is why it holds up across a mixed audience.

Does Jack customise it for our members?

Every time. Every booking starts with a 30-minute discovery call about who is in the room and what decision they are facing. No proposal goes out before that call.

Live or virtual?

Both. The virtual version runs live on Zoom or Teams and is rebuilt for the screen, not transferred from the stage.

Can members take something away?

Three questions to ask before any technology decision, and one first move for the week they get back. Short enough to repeat without a slide. A half-day workshop can follow the keynote for chapters that want to work a live decision.

Our date is close. Is it too late?

Ask. Jack keeps limited availability for short-notice dates and will say quickly whether the date and the room fit. Email jack@jackpflaherty.com directly.
